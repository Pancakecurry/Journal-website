# Motion Language

Motion should feel physical, gentle, and spatial. It communicates “I placed this somewhere,” not “the interface performed an animation.” Motion must clarify continuity, hierarchy, and state while remaining restrained.

## Vocabulary

### Place

An item moves into its intended position and settles. Use when a captured moment joins Today or an object is deliberately positioned.

### Lift

A paper or object elevates slightly when selected, dragged, or edited. Use to communicate active handling, not as constant hover ornament.

### Slide

A tab, divider, drawer, or neighboring surface moves into place while preserving spatial orientation.

### Turn

A notebook-like transition between major pages or temporal contexts. Use sparingly; it must not slow repeated navigation.

### Unfold

Additional information or the next reflection question is gradually revealed. The resulting layout should remain stable and understandable.

### Stamp

A quiet acknowledgement of meaningful completion, such as finishing an evening reflection. Never use it to score a Day or fuel a streak.

### Tuck

An item is bookmarked, filed, or placed away while leaving a clear sense of where it can be found again.

## Motion rules

- Prefer short, calm transitions with controlled easing over bouncy toy physics.
- Animate spatial relationships and state changes, not decoration for its own sake.
- Keep capture and writing responsive; motion must never delay saving, typing, or repeated actions.
- Avoid large parallax, surprise movement, looping ambient distraction, and motion that obscures status.
- Maintain input focus and reading position across transitions.
- Ensure animations remain coherent at mobile and desktop sizes.
- Never depend on animation alone to explain a state change.

## Reduced motion

Honor platform and in-product reduced-motion preferences. Replace large movement, page turns, parallax, and spring effects with immediate state changes or subtle crossfades where appropriate. Essential feedback must remain visible and understandable without motion.

Specific durations, easing curves, and implementation techniques are design-system and architecture decisions for later tasks.
