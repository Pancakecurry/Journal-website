# Product Vision

## Working identity

**Campus Atelier Journal** is an internal codename, not a final public brand. It must not be used to infer future naming, positioning, or cultural branding.

## The product

Campus Atelier Journal is a private, playful, local-friendly personal journaling environment where each calendar day becomes a collection of the user's thoughts, moments, photographs, moods, journal entries, reflections, tracker values, and memories.

The product should feel less like entering records into software and more like placing pieces of one's life into a beautifully maintained personal notebook.

> A private little world that gradually becomes more personal through use.

It should be beautiful, tactile, calm, warm, organized, slightly playful, and deeply personal—without becoming childish, scrapbook-maximalist, productivity-oriented, or AI-first.

## The primary object: the Day

The application fundamentally answers:

> What happened today?

The user's primary mental model is **the Day**, even when individual content types eventually have distinct internal models. A day may contain a sentence, a long entry, photographs, screenshots, moods, activities, achievements, arbitrary observations, tracker values, reflections, and custom information.

Recording must always be easier than categorizing. A user can write “Had ramen after class.” and save it without choosing a type or taxonomy. A day containing only that sentence is complete and valid.

## Core lifecycle

Open app → arrive at Today → optional morning check-in → add moments throughout the day → the Day gradually accumulates → optional evening wrap-up → the Day becomes history → revisit it through Calendar, Journal, or Memories.

The system must never imply that a day is incomplete because the user did not write enough or skipped a reflection.

## Long-term promise

The environment begins beautifully organized and relatively clean. Through authored content and meaningful use—not an unlock economy—it gradually becomes *their* beautifully organized environment.
