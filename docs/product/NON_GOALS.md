# Non-Goals

This product is not:

- A habit-streak machine, quantified-self scorecard, or gamified self-optimization product
- Productivity software wearing paper texture
- A generic journal database organized primarily by content type
- A generic dashboard, admin interface, or endless grid of cards and KPIs
- Beige SaaS with notebook lines
- Childish kawaii software or scrapbook maximalism
- A generic “Asian study aesthetic,” cultural imitation, or a place for fake Japanese/Korean writing
- Handwriting everywhere, stickers everywhere, or skeuomorphism for its own sake
- An AI wrapper or a journal that requires AI to work
- A social network, public publishing platform, or follower system
- An unlock economy involving XP, coins, gems, streak multipliers, battle passes, or energy

## Design patterns to avoid

- Oversaturated technology-brand colors, glowing gradients, and arbitrary glassmorphism
- Admin-dashboard sidebars, giant KPI tiles, and unnecessary pills
- Decorative animation without spatial meaning
- Excessive spring or bouncy toy physics
- Dense decoration that competes with writing
- Categories, forms, or surveys that slow simple capture
- Language that shames missed days, sparse entries, or skipped reflection

## Task 000 boundaries

This phase does not choose a frontend framework, runtime, database, server model, identity system, storage service, sync strategy, deployment provider, AI provider, encryption approach, PWA technology, or native wrapper. It does not scaffold an application, install packages, or implement production UI. The unresolved choices are tracked in the [architecture decision log](../architecture/DECISION_LOG.md).
