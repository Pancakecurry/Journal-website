# Campus Atelier v1.0 User Guide

## Start with today

Open Today and write what happened. A sentence is enough; categories are optional. Add a photograph, mood, longer journal entry, or reflection only when useful. Morning and evening prompts can always be skipped.

## Find your history

Calendar answers when something happened. Journal collects longer writing. Search finds locally available text. Historical Days are read-first but editable, and memories added later remain attached to when they happened.

## Make it yours

Personalization changes paper, cover, accent, density, and motion. My World becomes more inhabited from real journal history. Keepsakes and doodles attach back to a Day. Games are session-only play: there are no currencies, rewards, or streaks.

## Offline and installation

Visit Me → Install after loading the production app online once. A previously loaded install can reopen and write to local storage offline. Me → Storage shows browser storage information. Sync resumes after reconnecting when a remote account is configured.

## AI and privacy

Ordinary journaling and normal Search do not require AI. AI starts off. Me → Privacy & AI explains the provider boundary and lets you choose content scopes. Generated answers cite journal sources and may be wrong; your journal remains the source of truth.

## Own your data

Me → Export downloads readable Markdown, structured JSON, or a complete ZIP. Me → Restore inspects a supported archive before writing and preserves conflicts. Account deletion is deliberately separate from local-device cleanup so a failed remote deletion never pretends to have completed.
